Built on behavior science, not hacks

Pivot blends implementation intentions, emotional expression, and lightweight reflection to reduce friction between intention and action.

Implementation Intentions

Focus prompts improve follow-through

Pre-committing to one specific task increases the odds of doing it when distraction appears.

Expressive Writing

Venting reduces cognitive load

Brief emotional expression can reduce rumination and free attention for next actions.

Reflection Loop

Journal patterns sharpen future choices

Repeated, small reflections improve self-awareness and decision quality over time.

What Pivot does not claim

  • Pivot is not therapy or crisis care.
  • Pivot does not guarantee mental health outcomes.
  • Pivot supports habits; it does not replace professional support.
