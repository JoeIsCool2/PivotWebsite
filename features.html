Features designed for action, not endless tracking

Every feature exists to move users from impulse to intention in one short session.

Focus

What: one-task commitment + face-down execution timer.
When: attention starts fragmenting.
Outcome: measurable completion and streak momentum.

Vent

What: voice/text vent capture with supportive AI readout (Pro).
When: emotion blocks execution.
Outcome: faster cognitive reset and clearer next action.

Journal

What: timeline of Focus, Vent, and Move outcomes.
When: daily or weekly review.
Outcome: pattern awareness that improves decisions.

Move (performing strong)

Route-based movement with arrival confirmation, completion flow, and reflection capture. This flow is currently one of the strongest engagement drivers.

Pro

Adaptive Focus suggestions, richer Vent analysis, expanded themes/music, and weekly AI guidance.
