Privacy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

This page describes how the Pivot marketing website and the Pivot iOS app treat information in plain language. It is not legal advice. For App Store policies, Apple’s terms also apply.

This website

The static pages you are reading may be hosted by a provider (for example Netlify, Vercel, or Cloudflare). Those providers may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and operations. This site does not embed analytics by default; if you add analytics later, update this section.

The Pivot app (summary)

Pivot is designed for personal use on your iPhone. Journal entries, Focus sessions, Vent content, and Move activity are stored and processed primarily on your device. Some features may use Apple frameworks (such as speech recognition, maps, or on-device intelligence) according to Apple’s privacy rules.

If you use optional AI features that contact a server, only the data required for that request is sent, and you should review in-app disclosures and settings. Subscription purchases are handled by Apple via StoreKit.

Contact

For privacy questions about Pivot, contact the developer through the email or support channel listed on the App Store or TestFlight page when available.
