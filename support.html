Support

Need help with Pivot? Use the in-app help flow and quick answers below. No email required.

Get help in the app

Support is handled inside Pivot so you do not need a separate email workflow.

  1. Open Pivot on your iPhone.
  2. Go to Settings and open Help.
  3. Choose the topic and submit your question there.
  4. Check back in the app for your response and updates.

If you are blocked before entering the app, use the TestFlight link and install the latest build first.

Quick help

How do I join the beta?

Install Apple TestFlight, then open the invite link from the home page. If the beta is full, try again later.

How do I restore Pivot Pro?

Open the app settings and use the restore purchases option. Apple controls billing and receipt validation.

How can I report a bug well?

In the in-app help flow, include iPhone model, iOS version, what you tapped, and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.

Where can I read privacy details?

See our Privacy Policy for data handling, AI processing notes, and contact options.

Before you contact support:

  • Update to the latest TestFlight build.
  • Force close and reopen the app.
  • Restart your iPhone if a feature hangs.
