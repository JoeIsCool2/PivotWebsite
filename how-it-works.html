A reset loop that turns intent into action

Pivot is designed for the exact moment you notice you're scrolling without meaning to. Open the app, run one guided loop, then re-enter real life with momentum.

Start the loop See each feature
1. Focus

2. Vent

3. Journal

Capture pattern signals and choose what to repeat or change tomorrow.

When to use Pivot

  • After opening social apps without a reason
  • When stress is high and focus is low
  • When you need one small next step, not a life overhaul

What to expect in week one

  • Faster transition from overwhelm to action
  • A clearer pattern of your hardest times of day
  • More completed offline tasks with less friction
