A 4-step loop built for interruption moments

When users catch themselves scrolling unconsciously, Pivot gives them a psychologically clear sequence: commit, release, move, reflect.

Start the loop See each feature
1. Focus

Choose one task, then place phone face-down to protect attention and run the timer.

2. Vent

Type or speak what is stuck. Clearing cognitive load lowers resistance to action.

3. Move

Follow a real route, reach the marker, and complete with photo + reflection.

When people open Pivot

  • They notice a doom-scroll spiral starting
  • They feel mentally noisy and indecisive
  • They need momentum now, not motivation talk

Why this sequence works

  • Specific commitment beats vague intent
  • Emotional labeling reduces rumination
  • Movement completion creates fast reward and re-engagement
